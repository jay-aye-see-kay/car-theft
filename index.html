Car Theft labs

Procedural generation experiments. Real slices of Auckland at 1:1: the central city from Albert Park to Grafton, and the west coast from Piha to Kitekite Falls.

1. The semantic model

What the generators are given: roads with lane profiles, merged junctions, buildings with LiDAR heights, land areas and paths. No geometry decisions yet. Click a road to inspect it.

2. Road builder

Roads generated from the model: lane tapers, curved kerbs, footpaths, markings, bridges, and terrain graded to fit. Sliders regenerate it live.

3. Look study

The same tile under five looks: toon shading, ink outlines, shadows, trees placed by land type, shader windows, hill exaggeration. Includes a street-level drive camera.

4. Building grammar

Buildings near the view grown from footprint and height by a split grammar: floors, bays, windows, shopfronts, awnings, balconies, roofs. Seven styles picked by use and size.

5. Place profiles

The vibe of a place as data: each point resolves a stack of profiles (region, planning zone, mapped place, hand-drawn place, override) and every generator reads it. Profile and zoning maps, layer toggles, swaps, and a click inspector that says why. Switch to the Piha tile in its panel.

Data: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); LINZ elevation, Auckland 2024 (CC BY 4.0); Auckland Unitary Plan zones, Auckland Council (CC BY 4.0).
